# Label and Marking Control

Each released label shall include the approved product name, model/HVIN, serial number, input rating, hardware revision and authorized regulatory information.

Do not apply an invented FCC ID or IC number; a CE/UKCA mark before conformity assessment; or a UL/ETL mark without an active listing. Module identifiers may be used only when host-label and integration conditions are satisfied.

Label masters require controlled revision, Regulatory approval, first-article inspection, DHR scan verification and quarantine/destruction of obsolete stock.

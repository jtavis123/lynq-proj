# Regulatory Certification Matrix

**Planning document only. This is not a certificate or authorization to apply marks.**

| Market | Framework | LYNQ work before sale |
|---|---|---|
| United States | FCC equipment authorization and applicable Part 15 requirements | Determine certification/SDoC path; review modular approvals; test final-host emissions; assess RF exposure; approve labels/manuals through qualified lab/TCB |
| Canada | ISED RSS-Gen, applicable RSS and RSS-102 | Determine certification/SDoC path; test final product; control PMN/HVIN/FVIN/HMN; satisfy host/module and IC labeling rules |
| European Union | Radio Equipment Directive 2014/53/EU plus applicable safety, EMC and environmental obligations | Conformity assessment, risk analysis, technical file, test evidence and EU Declaration of Conformity before CE marking |
| United Kingdom | Applicable UK radio/product rules and PSTI consumer-connectable-product security requirements | Conformity evidence plus statement of compliance, no universal default passwords, vulnerability reporting route and published minimum security-update period |

Existing module approvals may reduce testing but do not automatically certify the assembled Hub or Node. Antenna, enclosure, power supply, co-located radios, firmware and host emissions can change the approval path.

# Cybersecurity and Update Compliance Notes

The deployment platform should be included in the product technical file and
risk assessment. It does not itself create regulatory certification.

Topics to document for production release include:

- Secure update design and release-key custody
- Software bill of materials
- Vulnerability intake and remediation policy
- Supported-update period
- Default-password prohibition
- Data minimization and crash-reporting consent
- EU Radio Equipment Directive cybersecurity requirements when applicable
- UK Product Security and Telecommunications Infrastructure requirements when
  applicable
- U.S. FCC equipment authorization impact of final radio/enclosure design
- ISED Canada equipment authorization impact
- Consumer IoT labeling programs if pursued

Final obligations must be reviewed by a qualified compliance laboratory and
legal counsel for each sales region.

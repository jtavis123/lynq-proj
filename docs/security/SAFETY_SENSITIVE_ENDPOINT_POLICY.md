# Safety-Sensitive Endpoint Policy

Safety-sensitive endpoint classes include, at minimum:

- Garage doors
- Vehicle gates
- Door locks
- Fireplaces
- Heaters
- Pumps with hazardous consequences
- Motorized equipment that could cause injury

## Default behavior

Remote and Alexa execution is denied until a product-specific policy has been
approved and configured. Local touchscreen control may also require confirmation.

## Future policy controls

- User confirmation
- PIN or voice code where supported
- Contact-sensor state validation
- Auto-close restrictions
- Time-of-day restrictions
- Audit history
- Installer classification
- Region-specific certification and legal review

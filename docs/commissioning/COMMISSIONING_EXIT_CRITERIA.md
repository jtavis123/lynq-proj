# Commissioning Exit Criteria

The first article may advance only when:

- Factory firmware backup is verified.
- Exact board revisions and dimensions are recorded.
- Hub and Node board profiles are evidence-based and locked.
- Every required commissioning test passes or is explicitly not applicable with
  engineering approval.
- No critical or high defect remains open.
- Ten-command IR or supported RF433 execution passes.
- Local operation works without internet.
- OTA rollback and configuration restore are demonstrated.
- Evidence package hashes verify.
- Quality approval is recorded.

Passing commissioning does not constitute FCC, ISED, CE, UKCA, UL or other
regulatory certification.

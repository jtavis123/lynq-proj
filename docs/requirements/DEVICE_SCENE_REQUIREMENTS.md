# Device and Scene Requirements

1. Devices shall have stable IDs independent of display names.
2. Devices shall be assignable to one room.
3. Device names, icons and room assignments shall be editable.
4. Alexa exposure shall be configurable per device and scene.
5. Safety-sensitive classification shall be stored per device.
6. Disabled devices shall not be exposed to Alexa or executed by scenes.
7. Scenes shall contain one or more ordered endpoint actions.
8. Scene actions shall execute through the Hub Command Router.
9. Scene execution shall report completed and failed action counts.
10. Device, room and scene configuration shall use a versioned persistent schema.
11. Configuration backup and restore shall be supported before commercial release.
12. Local operation shall not require cloud availability.

# Product Requirements

## Mandatory

1. The Hub shall control assigned Nodes over the local network.
2. Nodes shall learn and replay supported RF and IR commands.
3. Alexa commands shall route through the Hub to the correct Node.
4. Local touchscreen, schedule, and scene operation shall continue without internet access.
5. The Hub shall display Node online status and health information.
6. Firmware updates shall support signed OTA and rollback in production releases.

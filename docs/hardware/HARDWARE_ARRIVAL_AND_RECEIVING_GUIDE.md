# Hardware Arrival and Receiving Guide

## Do not power everything at once

Receive and inspect the Hub and each Node component before assembly or flashing.

## Photograph and record

For every item, photograph:

- Shipping label
- Manufacturer label and part number
- PCB front and back
- Connector sides
- Revision markings
- Included cables and accessories
- Any visible damage

## Verify the expected parts

### Hub
- Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-4.3B with case
- USB-C data cable
- Approved 5 V supply

### Each Rev A Node
- M5Stack AtomS3 Lite C124
- Atomic Port ABC A130
- RF433T U114
- RF433R U113
- IR Unit U002
- Factory Grove cables
- Enclosure and mounting hardware

Quarantine any substitute or damaged part. Do not modify an enclosure until the
first dry fit and dimensional record are complete.
